## ✍️ WRITING

### 1. The Email That Gets Replies

**Prompt:**
```
Write a professional email to [recipient role] about [topic].

Context:
- My role: [your role]
- Goal: [what you want them to do]
- Relationship: [cold/warm/existing]
- Tone: [friendly/formal/persuasive]

Requirements:
- Subject line that creates curiosity (not clickbait)
- Opening line that references THEIR priority, not mine
- One clear ask — make it easy to say yes
- Under 150 words
- End with a specific next step, not "let me know"
```

**Example:** "Write a professional email to a startup CEO about a potential AI automation project. My role: AI consultant. Goal: book a 15-minute call. Relationship: cold. Tone: friendly but professional."

**Expected Output:** A concise, action-oriented email with a compelling subject line, personalized opening, clear value proposition, and a specific CTA like "Would Thursday at 2pm work for a quick call?"

### 2. The Blog Post Outline Machine

**Prompt:**
```
Create a detailed blog post outline for the topic: "[topic]"

Target audience: [who]
Goal: [inform/persuade/educate/entertain]
Target length: [word count]

Include:
- A hook opening (story, statistic, or provocative question)
- 5-7 main sections with subpoints
- For each section: one data point or example to include
- SEO keywords to naturally weave in: [3-5 keywords]
- A conclusion with clear CTA
- 3 alternative title options (optimized for clicks + SEO)
```

**Expected Output:** A structured outline you can write (or have AI draft) in under 30 minutes, with built-in SEO optimization and engagement hooks.

## 💻 CODING

### 3. The Bug Whisperer

**Prompt:**
```
I have a bug in my [language/framework] code. Help me debug it.

**What should happen:** [expected behavior]
**What actually happens:** [actual behavior]
**Error message (if any):** [paste error]

**Code:**
```[language]
[paste your code]
```

Steps to reproduce:
1. [step 1]
2. [step 2]

What I've already tried:
- [attempt 1]
- [attempt 2]

Please:
1. Identify the root cause (not just the symptom)
2. Explain WHY it's happening
3. Provide the fix with explanation
4. Suggest how to prevent this type of bug in the future
```

**Expected Output:** A clear diagnosis with root cause analysis, corrected code with inline comments, and preventive best practices.

---

### 4. The Code Reviewer

**Prompt:**
```
Review this [language] code for production readiness:

```[language]
[paste code]
```

Evaluate on these dimensions (rate each 1-10):
1. **Correctness** — Does it do what it's supposed to?
2. **Security** — Any vulnerabilities?
3. **Performance** — Any bottlenecks or inefficiencies?
4. **Readability** — Would a new team member understand this?
5. **Error handling** — What happens when things go wrong?

For each issue found:
- Severity: 🔴 Critical / 🟡 Warning / 🟢 Suggestion
- Location: Line number or function
- Fix: Show the corrected code
```

**Expected Output:** A structured code review with severity ratings, specific line references, and ready-to-apply fixes.

## 🔬 RESEARCH

### 5. The Instant Research Assistant

**Prompt:**
```
I need to understand [topic] quickly and thoroughly.

Create a research brief that includes:

1. **Executive Summary** (3 sentences max)
2. **Key Concepts** — explain the 5 most important ideas, each in 2-3 sentences
3. **Current State** — what's happening right now (as of 2026)
4. **Key Players** — top 5 companies/people and why they matter
5. **Trends** — 3 emerging trends to watch
6. **Controversies** — what experts disagree about
7. **Numbers That Matter** — 5 key statistics
8. **Further Reading** — 5 recommended sources

Keep it factual. Flag anything you're uncertain about.
```

**Expected Output:** A comprehensive 1-page research brief that would take 3+ hours of manual research to compile.

### 6. The Decision Matrix Builder

**Prompt:**
```
Help me decide between these options: [Option A] vs [Option B] vs [Option C]

Context: [what this decision is for]
My priorities (ranked):
1. [most important factor]
2. [second factor]
3. [third factor]
4. [fourth factor]

For each option, evaluate:
- Score each priority 1-10 with brief justification
- List 3 pros and 3 cons
- Identify the biggest risk
- Estimate: cost, time to implement, difficulty (1-10)

Then:
- Create a weighted comparison table
- Give your recommendation with confidence level (high/medium/low)
- What would change your recommendation?
```

**Expected Output:** A structured decision matrix with weighted scores, clear trade-offs, and a defensible recommendation.

## 💼 BUSINESS

### 7. The Competitor X-Ray

**Prompt:**
```
Analyze [competitor name] as a competitor to my business.

My business: [brief description]
My target market: [who you serve]

Research and provide:
1. **What they do well** — 3 specific strengths
2. **Where they're weak** — 3 specific gaps or complaints
3. **Their pricing model** — how they charge and approximate rates
4. **Their marketing channels** — where they get customers
5. **Their messaging** — what promise do they make?
6. **Customer sentiment** — common praise and complaints
7. **Opportunity for me** — where can I win against them?

Be specific. Use concrete examples, not generic statements.
```

**Expected Output:** An actionable competitive intelligence brief that reveals specific opportunities to differentiate.

### 8. The Meeting Maximizer

**Prompt:**
```
I have a [meeting type] meeting in [timeframe].

Attendees: [who]
Topic: [what]
Goal: [desired outcome]
Duration: [time]

Create:
1. **Agenda** with time allocations (be ruthless — cut anything that could be an email)
2. **Pre-read** — 3-sentence summary to send attendees beforehand
3. **Key questions** to drive discussion (5 max)
4. **Decision framework** — how we'll make decisions if there's disagreement
5. **Action item template** — format for capturing next steps

Rule: If this meeting can be replaced by an async message, tell me that instead.
```

**Expected Output:** A tight, outcome-focused meeting agenda that respects everyone's time and ensures decisions get made.

## 🎨 CREATIVE

### 9. The Content Calendar Generator

**Prompt:**
```
Create a 2-week content calendar for [platform(s)].

Brand/Topic: [what you're about]
Target audience: [who]
Posting frequency: [how often per platform]
Content pillars: [3-4 themes you want to cover]
Tone: [professional/casual/witty/educational]

For each post provide:
- Day and platform
- Content type (text/image/video/carousel)
- Hook (first line that stops the scroll)
- Full caption/script
- Hashtags (platform-specific)
- CTA
- Best posting time

Also include:
- 1 "engagement bait" post (question/poll)
- 1 "value bomb" post (save-worthy tip)
- 1 "personal/behind-the-scenes" post
```

**Expected Output:** 14 days of ready-to-post content with captions, hashtags, and posting times — no more staring at a blank screen.
